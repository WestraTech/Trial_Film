Master Script  
**Act 1**  
**INT. A car is shown pulling up to a hiking trail**   
A car is shown pulling up and stopping with a ¼ view camera angle, the camera down on the ground. The car stops a few feet from the camera, and the doors open. 3 people step out of the car. The camera changes. Oliver has a football. He tosses it to Zach. They’re all laughing as they head into the woods. The camera shows their backs as it slowly pans to a “No Trespassing" sign that they seem to have missed.  
**Oliver**  
Ok guys, remember what we’re here for.  
**Zach**  
Morel Mushrooms.  
 **Oliver**  
Yo, we’re gonna make so much money from this.  
**Brandon**  
Yall brought your sacks?  
**Int. \- Oliver and Zach pull out their sacks to show Brandon.**  
**Oliver**  
(Holding up sack) Right here.  
**Brandon**  
And you got your cameras too, so we can vlog our mushroom collecting? Remember, we make money off of videos, too.  
**Oliver**  
It’s in my bag.  
**Zach**  
Mine’s in my bag too.  
**Int. \- Brandon nods and Friends 1 & 2 put their sacks back into their bag. Friends 1 & 2 keep passing the football back and forth. There is an overhead shot of the three of them heading deeper into the forest. The scene cuts to Oliver crouching down to what appears to be a group of mushrooms.**  
**Oliver**  
I found some mushrooms. They’re not the ones we’re looking for though.  
**Brandon**  
That’s alright, we can just keep looking.  
**Int. \- They stumble across a fork in the trail. Friends 1 & 2 are still passing the ball back and forth. Eventually, it gets passed into Zach’s hands.**  
**Zach**  
Yo Brandon, go long\!  
**Int. \- Zach throws the football down one of the paths, and Brandon’s attention quickly snaps to the ball and he chases after. The football lands in the thick underbrush, and Brandon has to take his time to try and find it.**  
**Brandon**  
(Calling over his shoulder) You suck at throwing\! And we’ve got more important things to do anyway.  
**Int. \- After a moment of searching, Brandon grabs the ball and begins walking back towards where his friends left him. He starts to realize they aren’t there and must have gone down one of the paths.**  
**Brandon**  
Ayo, which path did you guys take‽ (No response)  
**Brandon**  
(Muttering to himself) Dumasses.  
**Int. \- Brandon goes back along the path he came from towards where they were parked. There is an overhead shot showing the fork. You can see Brandon walking towards the car, and Friends 1 & 2 taking another path. The scene cuts to Friends 1 & 2 snickering.**  
**Zach**  
(Giggling) It’ll take him forever to catch up.  
**Oliver**  
Come on, let’s hurry.  
**Zach**  
What about the mushrooms?  
**Oliver**  
We can look for some as we go.  
**Zach**  
I kinda wish we still had the football.  
**Int. \- Brandon running out towards the car. He checks the front and back windows to see if they might be hiding inside. No one is there. He breathes a long, exasperated sigh before turning back for the woods. Once he makes it back to the fork, he walks down the correct trail this time. He pulls out the camcorder and begins recording.**  
**Brandon (Camcorder Footage)**  
What’s up guys, today we’re going to be showing you how to correctly pick Morel Mushrooms. Actually right now, it’s just me because my friends Oliver and Zach decided it would be funny to leave me behind. Anyway, our first step is to actually search for them. Now these guys are usually found next to or on dying trees. It’s also pretty good to search for them a couple days after it rains.
