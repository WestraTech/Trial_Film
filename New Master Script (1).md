Master Script  
**Act 1**  
**INT. A car is shown pulling up to a hiking trail**   
A car is shown pulling up and stopping with a ¼ view camera angle, the camera down on the ground. The car stops a few feet from the camera, and the doors open. 3 people step out of the car. The camera changes. Oliver has a football. He tosses it to Zach. They’re all laughing as they head into the woods. The camera shows their backs as it slowly pans to a “No Trespassing" sign that they seem to have missed.  
**Oliver**  
Ok guys, remember what we’re here for.  
**Zach**  
Morel Mushrooms.  
 **Oliver**  
Yo, we’re gonna make so much money from this.  
**Brandon**  
Y'all brought your sacks?  
**Int. \- Oliver and Zach pull out their sacks to show Brandon.**  
**Oliver**  
(Holding up sack) Right here.  
**Brandon**  
And you got your cameras too, so we can vlog our mushroom collecting? Remember, we make money off of videos, too.  
**Oliver**  
It’s in my bag.  
**Zach**  
Mine’s in my bag too.  
**Int. \- Brandon nods, and Oliver & Zach put their sacks back into their bag. Zach & Oliver keep passing the football back and forth. There is an overhead shot of the three of them heading deeper into the forest. The scene cuts to Oliver crouching down to what appears to be a group of mushrooms.**  
**Oliver**  
I found some mushrooms. They’re not the ones we’re looking for, though.  
**Brandon**  
That’s alright, we can just keep looking.  
**Int. \- They stumble across a fork in the trail. Zach & Oliver are still passing the ball back and forth. Eventually, it gets passed into Zach’s hands.**  
**Zach**  
Yo Brandon, go long\!  
**Int. \- Zach throws the football down one of the paths, and Brandon’s attention quickly snaps to the ball, and he chases after. The football lands in the thick underbrush, and Brandon has to take his time to try to find it.**  
**Brandon**  
(Calling over his shoulder) You suck at throwing\! And we’ve got more important things to do anyway.  
**Int. \- After a moment of searching, Brandon grabs the ball and begins walking back towards where his friends left him. He starts to realize they aren’t there and must have gone down one of the paths.**  
**Brandon**  
Ayo, which path did you guys take‽ (No response)  
**Brandon**  
(Muttering to himself) Dumbasses.  
**Int. \- Brandon goes back along the path he came from towards where they were parked. There is an overhead shot showing the fork. You can see Brandon walking towards the car, and Oliver & Zach taking another path. The scene cuts to Oliver & Zach snickering.**  
**Zach**  
(Giggling) It’ll take him forever to catch up.  
**Oliver**  
Come on, let’s hurry.  
**Zach**  
What about the mushrooms?  
**Oliver**  
We can look for some as we go.  
**Zach**  
I kinda wish we still had the football.  
**Int. \- Brandon running out towards the car. (Without the football; It’s in his backpack.) He checks the front and back windows to see if they might be hiding inside. No one is there. He breathes a long, exasperated sigh before turning back for the woods. Once he makes it back to the fork, he walks down the correct trail this time. He pulls out the camcorder and begins recording.**  
**Brandon (Camcorder Footage)**  
What’s up guys, today we’re going to be showing you how to correctly pick Morel Mushrooms. Actually right now, it’s just me because my friends Oliver and Zach decided it would be funny to leave me behind. Anyway, our first step is to actually search for them. Now these guys are usually found next to or on dying trees. It’s also pretty good to search for them a couple days after it rains. Sadly, they’re very rare, so they’re pretty hard to find.  
**Int. \- The scene now shows him from far behind, walking and talking to the camcorder. As he continues onward, a tree with a strange scratch mark slowly slips into view. A dramatic sound fades in.**  
**Act 1 End**

**Act 2**  
**Oliver**  
Yo, are those Morels?  
**Zach**  
(Stooping down) I think they are.  
**Oliver**  
Let me get out my camcorder. Brandon wanted us to record.  
(Oliver pulls out his Camcorder.)  
**Oliver \- Camcorder**  
Here, we’ve found some Morel mushrooms. You can see them up against this tree.  
**Zach \- Camcorder**  
They have these weird-looking holes in them. It almost looks like a walnut or something. (Camcorder zooms into the mushrooms.)  
**Oliver \- Camcorder**  
I’m going to gently pick them out of the soil and try to be careful with them. (Oliver pulls out his sack and places the Morel mushrooms into it.)  
**Zach \- Camcorder**  
These things are worth a decent amount of money, so if you find some, be sure to grab them. Or you could eat them later.  
**Oliver \- Camcorder**  
Personally, I’d sell them and then use the money to buy myself a huge steak.  
**Zach \- Camcorder**  
Yeah, I bet you would.  
**Oliver \- Camcorder**  
Hey, what’s that supposed to mean?  
**Zach**  
Why, nothing at all.  
**Oliver**  
Bullcrap.  
**Int. \- A tree suddenly shakes, as a flock of crows flees into the air from its branches. The sound startles Oliver and Zach, who quickly look up at the birds as they fly away.**  
**Zach**  
Well, that was weird.  
**Oliver**  
Could be that Brandon scared them away.  
**Zach**  
We should start moving again, or else he might catch up. (They look at each other, mischievously, before continuing down the trail.)  
**Int. \- The scene cuts back to Brandon, who now carries the camcorder by his side, not talking to it. He walks at a steady pace, following the trail that Oliver and Zach took.**  
**Brandon**  
Those two piss me off sometimes. (He picks up the pace.) Oliver\! Zach\!  
**Int. \- Oliver and Zach are walking. Brandon’s voice can be heard from far behind, shouting their names.**  
**Brandon**  
(From far away) Oliver\! Zach\!  
**Zach**  
Crap, he’s catching up.  
**Oliver**  
I have an idea… We need to go off-trail.  
**Zach**  
You’re right, he wouldn’t leave the path, would he?  
**Oliver**  
Quickly, this way\!  
**Int. \- Zach and Oliver quickly slip into the woods, venturing deeper and deeper to avoid Brandon. Oliver chuckles.**  
**Zach**  
Shh, you don’t want him to hear us\!  
**Oliver**  
(Hiding behind a tree) Let’s see if he passes us.  
**Brandon**  
(After a moment, calling) I can do this all day, you guys. (He passes them, continuing down the trail.  
**Oliver**  
(Whispering) Let’s go deeper.  
**Int. \- Brandon is shown continuing down the trail. As he travels, strange scratch marks begin to appear on the trees, as if something had been cutting through the bark.**  
**Brandon**  
That’s weird.  
**Brandon \- Camcorder**  
I know this is supposed to be a mushroom foraging video, but I think I just found bear marks on these trees. (Zoom in on the marks.) It’s cool, almost creepy to look at, but if there’s a bear lurking nearby, I’m shutting off the vlog for today and leaving. Or so you thought\! I could absolutely take a bear in a fight.  
**Int. \- Brandon notices something and points the camcorder towards the ground.**  
**Brandon**  
You see this mushroom?  
**Brandon \- Camcorder**  
This is called a false Morel. It looks almost identical to normal Morel shrooms, except it’s more red in color. These ones you don’t wanna eat. They can cause irreversible damage to your body. One thing about these guys is that the inside of them isn’t hollow like the Morel. It’s actually stuffed with these fibers, so there’s another way you can tell it’s not a Morel. Don’t cook them either. They’ll release poisonous fumes that can also do bad stuff to you. (Show Brandon kicking the mushroom.) We might not find any Morels today. If not, we’ll just come back later, but for now, let’s keep looking.  
**Int. \- Oliver and Zach are still walking off-trail through the woods. As they walk, trees begin to have scratches on them, but they don’t notice.**  
**Zach**  
Hey look\! (He points downwards)  
**Oliver**  
No way. More Morels already?  
**Zach**  
It appears so. This must be our lucky day. (He pulls out his sack and places the mushrooms in it.)  
**Oliver**  
Do we need to record this?  
**Zach**  
Well, it’s too late now. But have your camera out, just in case we run into any more.  
**Oliver**  
Alright. Let’s keep going too. We’re probably getting luckier since we’re farther from the trail.  
**Int. \- Oliver walks in front of Zach. As they continue forward, more scratches appear on trees. Zach finally takes notice.**  
**Zach**  
Are you seeing these, Oliver?  
**Oliver**  
Seeing what?  
**Zach**  
These scratches on the trees.  
**Oliver**  
(Looking up) Oh damn, you’re right.  
**Zach**  
These are mad weird. What do you think they came from? Raccoons or something?  
**Oliver**  
Nah, it’s gotta be something a little bigger. Those claw marks are definitely too big to be a raccoon.  
**Zach**  
So like a bear or something?  
**Oliver**  
I hope not. That would be bad news for us.  
**Zach**  
It could be bad news for the bear. There’s two of us.  
**Oliver**  
Yeah, and who’s to say there isn’t more than one of them?  
**Zach**  
That’s true. There are a lot of these scratches.  
**Int. \- Oliver stops suddenly, looking confused and startled. Zach bumps into him accidentally, before taking a step back.**  
**Zach**  
What are you doing bro.  
**Oliver**  
Hey Zach?  
**Zach**  
What?  
**Oliver**  
Look up for a second.  
**Zach**  
(As he looks up, his eyes widen a bit. Oliver holds up his camcorder and starts recording.) Now *that* is weird.  
**Act 2 End**

**Act 3**  
**Int. \- Brandon is continuing down the trail, his eyes scouring the ground for mushrooms. Some of the trees around him still have scratches on them. Some scratches are in X patterns; others are just slashes. Finally, after a moment, he sees something.**  
**Brandon**  
(Aiming camcorder) Finally, actual Morels. This is great\!
